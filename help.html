How the route check works

Before every trip we send you the route to look over. It takes about two minutes, and you do it on your phone.

1

Open the link

We send you a short link on WhatsApp. Tap it and the route opens in your phone.

No app to install, no account, no password.

2

Read the route

You see the trip day by day: where the guests sleep, what they do, and in which order the days come.

3

Change what is wrong

Every day has its own small buttons. This is what they do:

▲▼ Move a whole day up or down. Use these to put the days in a better order. Tap ▲ once and that day swaps places with the one above it; tap it again to move it further up. They turn pale at the top and the bottom of the trip, because there is nowhere further to go.
✕ Take something out of a day — an activity that will not work, a lodge that is wrong.
↩ Put it back if you removed it by mistake. Nothing is lost.

The dates do not travel with the day. Day 3 stays day 3 — what changes is what happens on it. So if you move the Bwindi day up, Bwindi simply happens earlier.

Some days are tied to a fixed date, like a gorilla permit. You can still move them, and your answer will carry a note so we check that one together.

After a change, write one line to say why. That line matters as much as the change itself: it tells us what to do differently on the next trip.

4

Add anything else

At the bottom there is a box for everything that does not belong to one day: a road to avoid, a lodge you heard good things about, a time that will not work.

5

Send it back

One button at the bottom of the page. It opens WhatsApp with your answer already written. Press send, and you are done.

Good to know

  • Nothing is sent until you press that button. You can close the page and start again whenever you want.
  • Your changes stay on your phone until you send them. We see nothing before that.
  • Once the page is open it keeps working without internet, so a weak signal on the road is not a problem.
  • Each link stays open for about a month.
  • There are no prices and no guest names on the page. Only the route.

If anything goes wrong, just send us a WhatsApp message. There is no wrong way to answer, and you cannot break anything. Tell us in your own words and we will sort it out.
